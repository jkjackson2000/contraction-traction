Privacy Policy

Contraction Traction (“Contractions”) for iPhone · Effective October 18, 2026

Short version: we collect nothing. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to share it, and is never sent to us.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting of its own. It contains no networking code and never connects to a server.

Data stored on your device

The contraction times, durations, and notes you record are stored on your device, in the app's private storage, and are included in your device or iCloud backups like other app data. They are never sent to us. Your settings are stored the same way. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete All Data in the app, removes them.

Sharing

Apart from your own backups, data leaves your device only when you explicitly share it. When you tap Share, the app creates a plain-text log (or a .txt file of the same text) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (for example Messages, AirDrop, or Notes) is your choice and is governed by that service. When you import, the text you paste or open is read on your device only.

Live Activity

The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island timer is drawn by iOS on your device from information the app provides locally. No push notifications or servers are involved.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
