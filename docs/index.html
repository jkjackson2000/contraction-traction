Contraction Traction

A free, private contraction timer for iPhone. Shown on your Home Screen as “Contractions”.

Timing tool, not medical advice. Follow your provider's instructions. In an emergency call 911.

What it does

  • One big Start/Stop button times each contraction, with how long it lasted and how far apart they are.
  • Last-hour averages, a timeline of the last two hours, and an optional 5-1-1 (or 4-1-1, 3-1-1) pattern note.
  • “Log past” adds contractions from memory in a couple of taps. Approximate entries are marked with “~”.
  • A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Start/Stop button.
  • Partners can share their log as plain text (Messages) or a .txt file (AirDrop). The other phone pastes or opens it, reviews the merge, and imports it. Nothing is deleted, and Undo restores the previous list.

Privacy

Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, or network access. See the privacy policy.

Help

Import says “No contractions recognized”. Paste the whole message shared from the app. Each entry line starts with a date, for example 2026-10-18 2:10:03 PM · 58s. Hand-typed lines like 2026-10-18 14:10 | ~1m also work.

The Live Activity doesn't appear. Check that Live Activities are on in Settings › Contractions and in the app's own Settings, then tap Start.
